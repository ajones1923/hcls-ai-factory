## Tips

- Numbered-timeline infographics with this much text are the hard case — if labels crowd or
  misspell, regenerate with: *"keep the exact 0–4 timeline layout but regenerate only the text
  so every label is sharp and correctly spelled."*
- All figures are truthful to the build and the runbook (`docs/TSC_DEMO_RUNBOOK.md`). Keep the
  SYNTHETIC / decision-support / not-FDA-cleared labels — they are what make it credible.
- The Node-4 tagline and the Act stats are interchangeable; pick the ones that land hardest
  with the room.
