# Storyboard — deck recipes for the TSC AI Engine

How to sequence the infographics (and which documents to hand out) for specific audiences. Each
recipe lists the order, *why* each piece sits where it does, the document to leave behind, the one
number to lead with, and what to avoid. Generate the images from the prompts in this folder, then
arrange them per recipe.

## Asset shorthand

**Infographics** (this folder, numbered `01`–`18` in walkthrough order — recipes below use the
short names; the number is the file prefix): `01` summary/executive one-pager · `02` biology
(mTOR) · `03` problem_solution · `04` engine7_hero · `05` engine7_portrait · `06`
engine7_landscape · `07` architecture_ai_tiering · `08` patient_journey · `09` mosaic_recovery ·
`10` omniverse_four_scenes · `11` omniverse_pipeline · `12` omniverse_hero · `13`
validation_scorecard · `14` trust_layer · `15` engine7_demo_flow · `16` cincinnati_roadmap ·
`17` generalization · `18` small_cheap_open.

**Handout docs** (`../`): `TSC_DEMO_RUNBOOK`, `TSC_ENGINE_LEARN_GUIDE` (8–9th grade),
`TSC_ENGINE_LEARN_GUIDE_ADVANCED` (12th), `TSC_ENGINE_OMNIVERSE_VISUALS_GUIDE`,
`TSC_INTELLIGENCE_ENGINE_RESEARCH_PAPER` / `_PRD`, `TSC_DIGITAL_TWIN_RESEARCH_PAPER` / `_PRD`,
`TSC_INTELLIGENCE_ENGINE_PEDIATRIC_IMPACT`, `TSC_INTELLIGENCE_ENGINE_CCHMC_HANDOVER`.

## Universal rules (apply to every recipe)

- **Lead with the audience's pain, not your tech.** Open with `problem_solution` or
  `mosaic_recovery` for clinical audiences; with `engine7_hero` only for execs/keynotes.
- **Always pair a number with its caveat.** Whenever you show `validation_scorecard`, say the
  construct-validity line out loud. The honesty is the credibility.
- **Close on the ask or the road ahead**, never on a feature.
- **Tailor the one lead number** to the room (noted per recipe).

## Recipe 1 — Cincinnati Children's / Dr. Hagedorn (the meeting that matters)

*Goal: earn the retrospective biobank study. Audience: a CHIO + TSC clinicians. Tone: clinical-first,
humble, "let's test it on your specimens" — not a vendor pitch.*

1. **`problem_solution`** — open on *their* world: the six struggles. Signals you understand TSC care
   before you show any technology.
2. **`mosaic_recovery`** — the clinical hook, and it points straight at their biobank tissue.
3. **`engine7_landscape`** — briefly, *what* the engine is (so it reads as a real system, not a slide).
4. **`omniverse_four_scenes`** *(or the live twin)* — show, don't tell — but grounded, right after the
   substance.
5. **`validation_scorecard`** — the numbers *and* the honest "construct validity, not clinical validity"
   caveat. A CHIO trusts the caveat more than the numbers.
6. **`cincinnati_roadmap`** — close on the ask: the retrospective NMI study on already-banked tissue, with
   the five touchpoints and the biobank starred.

**Leave behind:** `CCHMC_HANDOVER` + `PEDIATRIC_IMPACT` + `LEARN_GUIDE_ADVANCED`.
**Lead number:** *"6 of 6 mosaics recovered — diagnoses your blood tests would have missed."*
**Avoid:** opening with Omniverse flash; any hint of overclaiming clinical validity.

## Recipe 2 — the platform executive review (Aaron / leadership)

*Goal: position this as a strategic HCLS go-to-market proof asset. Tone: concise, business value.*

1. **`engine7_hero`** — the one-glance opener.
2. **`problem_solution`** — the market problem this addresses in HCLS.
3. **`validation_scorecard`** — it works, and we measure it (credible = sales-ready).
4. **`omniverse_hero`** *(or `omniverse_four_scenes`)* — the differentiated wow: Omniverse precision-
   medicine visuals authored on a Spark, rendered on RunPod (a natural NVIDIA + the platform story).
5. **`cincinnati_roadmap`** — named-institution traction (a CHIO engagement) = the GTM proof point.

**Talking point to add (no infographic needed):** *small, cheap, open, and reusable* — one $4,699 Spark,
Apache 2.0, and the "swap the box labels" generalization to NF1/NF2/Rett. That is the platform thesis.
**Leave behind:** `DEMO_RUNBOOK` + the research-paper exec summary.
**Lead number:** *"A named CHIO engagement and a +12-point diagnostic uplift — on a $4,699 box."*

## Recipe 3 — Conference talk / keynote (technical + visionary)

*Goal: impress and inspire — the future of edge precision medicine. Tone: high energy, wow then substance.*

1. **`engine7_hero`** — title slide.
2. **`mosaic_recovery`** — open on a human story (the missed diagnosis).
3. **`engine7_landscape`** — the "five agents" architecture reveal.
4. **`omniverse_pipeline`** — the clever bit: author on the Spark (CPU, free), render on RunPod (RTX).
5. **`omniverse_four_scenes`** → **`omniverse_hero`** — the visual crescendo (pair with a live twin scrub).
6. **`validation_scorecard`** — "...and it's measured, honestly" — the integrity beat that lands after the wow.
7. **`engine7_demo_flow`** — recap the three-act arc as the close / call to action.

**Leave behind:** `OMNIVERSE_VISUALS_GUIDE` + `DIGITAL_TWIN_RESEARCH_PAPER`.
**Lead with:** the live twin scrub and *"the render can't lie — the cloud's size is the prediction interval."*

## Recipe 4 — Families / patient advocacy

*Goal: hope and understanding, in plain language. Tone: warm, accessible, honest — never overpromising.*

1. **`problem_solution`** — name the everyday hardships families recognize.
2. **`mosaic_recovery`** — the hope: ending the diagnostic odyssey.
3. **`omniverse_four_scenes`** — families respond to *seeing* it; the body atlas and the glowing cells land.
4. **`validation_scorecard`** — be honest that it is not yet tested on real patients. Families deserve, and
   respect, that candor.
5. **`cincinnati_roadmap`** — "here is how it could reach a child like yours."

**Leave behind:** `LEARN_GUIDE` (8–9th grade) + `PEDIATRIC_IMPACT`.
**Lead with:** the diagnostic-odyssey hope. **Avoid:** jargon and any promise of a cure or a timeline.

## Recipe 5 — Research / scientific collaborators

*Goal: rigor, credibility, collaboration. Tone: precise, methods-forward, honest.*

1. **`problem_solution`** — frame the unmet clinical need.
2. **`engine7_portrait`** — the full architecture, in depth.
3. **`mosaic_recovery`** — the central hypothesis (mosaic recovery from tissue).
4. **`validation_scorecard`** — the methods and the construct-vs-clinical-validity distinction — this
   audience cares most about exactly this.
5. **`cincinnati_roadmap`** — the proposed prospective study and the collaboration.

**Leave behind:** `RESEARCH_PAPER` + `LEARN_GUIDE_ADVANCED`.
**Lead with:** the methodology and the honest validation framing.
