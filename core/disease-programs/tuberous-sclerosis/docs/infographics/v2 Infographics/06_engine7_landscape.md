# Slide 06 — How the data flows

## 🎙️ Narration script (~55 sec)

Let's follow the data through it, left to right. [beat]

It starts with the synthetic cohort on the left. The first stop is always the Phenome Mapper —
it's the foundation, because everything downstream reads the phenotype it builds. From there the work
fans out, in parallel, to three agents: the Variant Curator on the genetics, the Trajectory Modeler
on the forecasts, and the TAND agent on the behavioral signals. Those three then converge into the
Therapeutics Strategist, which assembles the final options brief.

Underneath, the orchestrator coordinates all of it and routes the results to the clinician's
surfaces — the briefing, the dashboard, the disciplined alerts, and the digital twin. And running
along the bottom: provenance on every output, and the measured scorecard. [beat] It's a clean
pipeline — data in, a clear picture out.
