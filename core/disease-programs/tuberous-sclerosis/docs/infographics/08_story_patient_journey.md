Create **FRAME 08 — ONE PATIENT, END TO END**: a warm, human **left-to-right journey** — a glowing soft-gold path carrying one child's data from the patient (left bookend: a child) through the five agents (on the orchestrator spine) to the surfaces/twin and the clinician's decision (right bookend: gold). Warmer and more human than the other diagrams — emphasize the two human bookends. (Visually distinct from the numbered process-timeline of frame 15.)

**TOP HEADER (full width):** Title **"One Patient, End to End"**, subtitle **"How a child's data flows through the TSC Intelligence Engine"**; small pink pill top-right **"SYNTHETIC patient · not FDA-cleared"**.

**START (far left) — "THE PATIENT" (child icon):** *"A child with TSC: genomic data, longitudinal records, clinical notes, prior imaging."*

**Then FIVE agent stages along the path, each a card with an icon, the agent name, and what it contributes for this patient:**
1. **"Phenome Mapper"** *(runs first)* — *"Builds the ontology-grounded phenotype map every other agent reads."*
2. **"Variant Curator"** — *"Recovers the genetic variant (including a low-VAF mosaic from tissue) and classifies it under ACMG."*

5. **"Therapeutics Strategist"** — *"Assembles a six-section, source-attributed options brief."*

**Then "THE ORCHESTRATOR" (a thin spine the stages sit on):** *"coordinates the agents in order and logs every step."*

**Then "THE SURFACES + TWIN" (cluster of small screen icons + a glowing mini 3-D twin):** *"a pre-visit briefing, the in-visit dashboard, disciplined alerts, and the 3-D digital twin."*

**END (far right, gold) — "THE DECISION":** *"the clinician — now holding the whole picture, with the math done and every claim sourced — makes a better-informed, better-timed decision."*

**BOTTOM CALLOUT BAND (gold-bordered):** **"Five specialists' worth of analysis, assembled into one clear picture — for one child, in one visit."**

**FOOTER (thin):** *"Apache 2.0 · Engine 7 of the HCLS AI Factory · SYNTHETIC demonstration data · decision support, clinician review required · not FDA-cleared."*

Composition: a clear, warm left-to-right journey from "the patient" through the five agents (on the orchestrator spine) to the surfaces/twin and finally the clinician's decision in gold. Human and credible, not a cartoon. Every label sharp and readable.
