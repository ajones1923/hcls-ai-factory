### Why this matters for the platform + NVIDIA positioning

1. **Imaging** is a well-defined, high-conviction market with clear FDA pathways, established MONAI/NIM tooling, and acute workforce shortages driving adoption. It is the proof-of-concept wedge.

2. **Biomarkers** represent a significantly larger addressable market but are more fragmented across precision medicine, pharmacogenomics, longevity, and liquid biopsy. The Biomarker Intelligence Agent consolidates these under a single canonical data model — the same architectural pattern (canonical artifact + three-plane AI platform) that makes the Imaging Agent compelling.

3. **Cross-modal integration** (imaging findings triggering genomic workflows, biomarker trajectories prompting confirmatory imaging) is where the HCLS AI Factory creates value that no single-agent market sizing captures. This is the platform premium.
