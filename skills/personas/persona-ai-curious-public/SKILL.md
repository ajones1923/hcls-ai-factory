# The AI-Curious Public — the reach engine

The broad non-specialist scrolling a feed or skimming a headline. They will never read the code,
but they are the amplifier: they share widely, project large hope, and turn a wave of attention
into the forks and collaborators that actually move the mission. Their reaction is the top of the
funnel for everyone else.

## What wins them / What loses them

**Wins them:** one legible, jaw-dropping demonstration made human — *"a genome becomes a candidate
molecule, on one box, open to the world"* — plus the hook that it belongs to them: **"and it's
yours."** The shock is not "AI did this," it's "AI did this, and you can have it." Legibility beats
detail; they need to *feel* the leap, not follow it.

**Loses them / the risk:** over-reading. This audience most easily hears "AI cures disease now,"
and that misread doesn't stay contained — it spills into hype that detonates with experts and arms
critics elsewhere. The failure mode is not indifference; it's a viral overclaim you didn't write
but did invite.

## How to speak to them

- Lead with **one clear wonder**, not a feature list: raw DNA reads going in, a real candidate
  molecule against a real target coming out, on a single affordable machine.
- Land the **"and it's yours"** hook — open-source, Apache-2.0, free to run, free to build a
  business on. That line is the project's alone and it is what makes the wow *matter*.
- Bound the wonder **inside** the excitement, not as a buried caveat: "candidate molecules for
  researchers to test," "decision support for doctors — not a diagnosis." One honest clause keeps
  the whole thing credible and pre-empts the misread.
- Use the **mission cut** — human stakes, understated. A single quiet, true sentence about the
  stakes carries further than a montage and immunizes against the "hype" read.
- When you invoke the mission, use the real line plainly: *no one should die of a disease we could
  have understood in time.* Say it once, straight, and stop.

## Do / Don't

**Do:** make one thing legible and astonishing; pair every wow with the "it's yours" hook; keep an
honest clause visible; point clearly to where the wonder lives (the repo). **Don't:** let a leap
imply "cured / diagnosed / available at your clinic today"; stack superlatives; chase spectacle
past what the honesty ledger allows; use a sick patient as a prop.
