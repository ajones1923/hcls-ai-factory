A patient's DNA in the morning. A hundred ranked molecule candidates by that afternoon.

The HCLS AI Factory is an open-source precision medicine platform — eight compute engines, eight intelligence agents, one platform — running on a single accelerated box that sits on a desk. These videos walk the architecture in detail, then two complete workflows end to end. Every number on screen comes from code you can clone and read.

What this is, and what it isn't

The molecules

Designs shown are computational candidates, generated and ranked against a target pocket. They are not finished medicines and no wet-lab validation is claimed.

The clinical work

Everything clinical is decision support for a qualified clinician — never autonomous diagnosis, never prescribing.

Labelled honestly

The platform marks what it did not do: cached results say cached, models that did not run say not run. That constraint is in the architecture, not bolted on at the end.

Build state

The first engines are online and the rest are being added; the videos call out planned work by name. The maturity matrix is the current record of what is live, verified, gated or planned.
