<section class="hcls-watch__limits">
  <h2>What this is, and what it isn't</h2>
  <div class="hcls-watch__grid">
    <div class="hcls-watch__limit">
      <h3>The molecules</h3>
      <p>Designs shown are <b>computational candidates</b>, generated and ranked against a target pocket. They are not finished medicines and no wet-lab validation is claimed.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="hcls-watch__limit">
      <h3>The clinical work</h3>
      <p>Everything clinical is <b>decision support for a qualified clinician</b> — never autonomous diagnosis, never prescribing.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="hcls-watch__limit">
      <h3>Labelled honestly</h3>
      <p>The platform marks what it did not do: cached results say cached, models that did not run say <b>not run</b>. See the <a href="/honesty/">Honesty &amp; Governance</a> section for the full ledger.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="hcls-watch__limit">
      <h3>Build state</h3>
      <p>The first engines are online and the rest are being added; the videos call out planned work by name. The <a href="/honesty/maturity-matrix/">maturity matrix</a> is the current record.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
